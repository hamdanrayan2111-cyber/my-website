About me

Organised, optimistic, determined, flexible and rigorous.

As part of ARTIS Multimédia, I help run digital workshops and organise activities for young people. I teach office software to beginners, mentor interns and contribute to the development of various projects, including the Erasmus+ programme.

Before moving into education and community work, I worked as a low-voltage and network technician: structured cabling, Wi-Fi access point installation and network rack connections. This mix of technical and teaching skills lets me adapt to any environment and always find the right solution.
